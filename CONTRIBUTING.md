# Contributing

Contributions are welcome through [Issues](https://github.com/gusgustavodj/kontrol-s8-protocol/issues) and [Pull Requests](https://github.com/gusgustavodj/kontrol-s8-protocol/pulls). Use Issues for protocol findings, documentation corrections, technical clarification, or compatibility reports. Keep reports concise and distinguish observed facts from interpretation. Describe device/firmware and test setup only when known. Do not upload private information, proprietary captures, raw USB/HID captures, or material you lack rights to publish.

Pull Requests should explain the proposed change, motivation, affected files, evidence or tests, related Issue, and any licensing or third-party material concerns.

Contributions are welcome. All pull requests are reviewed by the maintainer, who decides whether changes are incorporated into the official repository. Issues and Pull Requests do not obligate implementation, acceptance, or merging. The maintainer may request changes, accept a proposal, or close it without merging. Lawful reuse under the applicable licenses remains governed by those licenses.
