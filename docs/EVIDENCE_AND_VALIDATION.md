# Technical evidence and practical validation

| Evidence layer | What it shows | What it does not show |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Original Traktor captures | Observed messages and field interpretation | That a later mapping was sent by Traktor |
| Atlas and tables | Addressing, interfaces and consolidated data | Physical validation of every record |
| Windows/Android mappings | Independent application of knowledge on real hardware | A universal normative USB architecture |
| Owner statement | Physical HID INPUT, LED and Display PASS in tested configurations | All commands, builds, operating systems and audio channels |
| Photo or video | Complementary visual demonstration | Exact bytes of the original captures |

`DISPLAY_MAPPING_PHYSICAL_PASS` and `ALL_TRAKTOR_STARTUP_BYTES_DOCUMENTED` are independent states. The first is owner-attested PASS; the second is incomplete.
